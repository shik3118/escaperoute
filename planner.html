YOUR TRIP, STEP BY STEP

Plan with confidence.

01

Choose your destination

Think about the scenery, activities and atmosphere you want. Check the season and local conditions before you decide.

02

Pick your dates

Consider the weather, transport schedules and how much time you have available.

03

Set a budget

Estimate transport, accommodation, food and activity costs. Keep a little extra for changes.

04

Plan your route

Check travel times and connections. Leave room for unexpected changes rather than filling every minute.

05

Pack the essentials

Prepare suitable clothing, documents, medication and a phone charger according to your own needs.

06

Review before departure

Confirm important details and check current local travel information before leaving.
